Important disclaimer: This classroom game is a simplified teaching model for explanation and time-restraint purposes. It does not fully represent real banking, Swiss or international bank regulation, central-bank policy, minimum reserve rules, capital and liquidity requirements, deposit insurance, interbank lending, lender-of-last-resort support, risk-weighted assets, loan defaults, accounting rules, market liquidity, legal constraints, customer behaviour, or longer-term loan contracts. Numbers, returns, trust changes, collapses, and outcomes are fictional and designed to show the core trade-off between profitability, liquidity, risk, and trust.
